# Artwork

These original illustrations were created for this project with the built-in
image-generation tool. The terrain, interface, coin indicator, and layout are
drawn by the game; the PNG illustrations are the following generated assets:

- `house.png`: cheerful mint-blue house with a coral roof and cream trim,
  isolated as a 3/4 isometric cartoon sprite on a transparent background.
- `cafe.png`: cheerful teal café with a coral-and-cream awning and coffee-cup
  sign, isolated as a 3/4 isometric cartoon sprite on a transparent background.
- `bakery.png`: cheerful peach-brick bakery with a blue-green roof and bread
  sign, isolated as a 3/4 isometric cartoon sprite on a transparent background.
- `tree.png`: a rounded jade-green tree, isolated as a cartoon sprite on a
  transparent background.
- `milo.png`: a cheerful gardener in a mustard cap and blue overalls with a
  watering can, isolated as a full-body cartoon character on transparency.
- `nora.png`: a cheerful café owner in a teal dress and coral apron holding a
  coffee cup, isolated as a full-body cartoon character on transparency.
- `pip.png`: a cheerful older baker in a white hat and peach apron carrying a
  bread basket, isolated as a full-body cartoon character on transparency.
- `icon.png`: a square illustrated city launcher icon featuring a turquoise
  house, café, trees, and a floating gold coin, without lettering.

All illustrations were requested with an original, warm, hand-painted cartoon
style; no third-party game characters or logos were used as inputs.
